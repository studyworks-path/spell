              How it works

              Start here

              Tap Start review once a day. It brings back terms just before you'd forget them, plus a few new ones in lecture order. Five to ten minutes a day is plenty.

              Every term climbs five steps

              1. New term shown with its description
              2. Dashes and first letter
              3. Dashes only
              4. No dashes, type the whole term
              5. Test conditions: no dashes, 30 seconds

              Spell it right and next time there's less help. A slip moves that term back one step, never to the start. The coloured bar on each card shows its step.

              Typing

              Tap the letters on the app's keyboard. To fix a letter, tap that dash first. Stuck? Give me a letter or Show answer, and the term comes back to practise. Only terms you spell yourself count toward your daily goal.

              Practice and games

              Under Practice you can work through one lecture, mix them all, or use flashcards. Lectures at silver are marked ready in the games. Time critical at 30 seconds a patient matches the real test.

              Badges and levels

              Bronze: half a lecture learned. Silver: all of it learned. Gold: every term test-ready. Your level grows from Pathbaby all the way to Pathophreak.

              Your progress is saved on this phone, in this browser. There's no sign-in, and nothing is sent anywhere.

              Settings

              "Steps up as you learn" gives each term less help as you get it right: a flashcard first, then dashes with the first letter, dashes only, no dashes, and finally 30 seconds like the real test. A slip moves that term back one step.
